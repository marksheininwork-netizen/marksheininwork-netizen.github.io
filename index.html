IdeaCircuit Studio is a private tool for creating and publishing technology and AI explainer videos to The Idea Circuit’s own YouTube channel.

This is an owner-operated application. It does not offer public accounts or a sign-in service for visitors.

What the application does

The production workflow combines authored explanations, licensed moving footage, original motion graphics, narration and captions. Rendered videos are checked before publishing.

The YouTube integration verifies the owner’s channel, uploads a video privately, checks processing, and can schedule that same upload for release. Publishing receipts help prevent duplicate uploads.

Why it connects to YouTube

The channel owner authorizes the application through Google OAuth. The application uses that authorization to identify the owner’s channel, upload videos and metadata, inspect upload status, and manage scheduled releases. The site itself has no Google sign-in form.
