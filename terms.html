Terms of use

IdeaCircuit Studio is a private, owner-operated production and publishing tool for The Idea Circuit.

Private-use scope

This website provides information about the application. It does not offer public accounts, paid subscriptions, a public upload service or authorization to another person’s channel. Visiting the site does not grant access to the production workflow.

Owner authorization

The channel owner controls which videos are uploaded, scheduled or published. The application uses the Google permissions granted by that owner. Only content that the owner is entitled to use should enter the workflow.

Third-party services

Use of YouTube, Google APIs and GitHub is subject to their respective applicable terms, including the YouTube Terms of Service. Rendering and other providers may have their own usage limits and licensing requirements. Publication and availability depend on those services.

Data practices

The application’s current handling of account information, publishing records and media is described in the privacy policy.
